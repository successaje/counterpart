# Code of conduct

Counterpart is a small project with an open door. We expect everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, rooms and reviews, to be considerate, direct and patient.

- Disagree with ideas, not people. Explain what you would do instead.
- Assume good faith. A terse review comment is usually a busy reviewer, not a hostile one.
- Keep evidence honest. Do not post redacted output as if it were raw, or raw output as if it were redacted.
- Respect the gate. Never ask anyone, in a room or a review, to approve something they have said no to.

Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated. If something happens, write to the maintainer listed in `package.json`. Reports are handled privately; the outcome may be a warning, a removal from the discussion, or a ban.

This document is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and kept short so that it is read.
